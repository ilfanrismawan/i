# Plain-language script contract — Indonesian, understandable around age 7

Write every viewer-facing narration in **bahasa Indonesia sederhana yang dapat dipahami anak sekitar 7 tahun**, while staying interesting and respectful to adults. This is a language-complexity target, not permission to introduce false analogies.

- Open with a familiar observation and a curiosity question. Explain **one idea at a time** with short, spoken sentences (aim 7–14 words, not a rigid limit).
- Prefer everyday words. Replace or immediately explain jargon: `elektroda` → `bagian kecil penghantar listrik`; `kapasitansi` → `perubahan kecil pada hubungan listrik yang bisa diukur`; `induksi elektromagnetik` → `perubahan medan magnet yang menghasilkan listrik di kumparan lain`.
- If a technical term is essential, introduce it *after* the understandable meaning and use it sparingly. No unexplained acronyms or formula dumps.
- Use one concrete analogy at a time; label analogy as an analogy when it is not physically literal. Never say current travels through an intact screen glass into the finger.
- Use story progression: familiar event → question → what happens unseen → cause and effect → simple conclusion. Avoid repetitive hype, fear bait, exaggerated certainty, CTA.
- Read the narration aloud and revise awkward sentences. Give a glossary for the production team **outside** the spoken script, not overlays in Blender.
- Verify scientific claims first. Simplify **words**, never distort facts. Keep caveats in plain language when they materially matter.
- Proposed duration is approximate until real voice-over is recorded. After VO, use audio-based alignment for captions and final cut timing.

## Script review checklist
1. Could an average seven-year-old follow the sequence if shown the animation?
2. Does each sentence convey one idea?
3. Are jargon and acronyms absent or briefly explained?
4. Can every cause-and-effect step be backed by approved evidence?
5. Is the ending a satisfying explanation without a CTA?
6. Is the narration spoken naturally, not written like a textbook?

## Example
Too technical: `Sensor mutual-capacitance mengukur perturbasi kopling elektroda TX/RX untuk mengestimasi koordinat.`
Better: `Di balik kaca ada bagian kecil yang bisa merasakan perubahan listrik. Saat jarimu mendekat, perubahan itu membantu iPhone tahu bagian layar mana yang kamu sentuh.`
Do not use this simplified example as evidence of Apple-specific internals; cite technology-specific evidence separately.
